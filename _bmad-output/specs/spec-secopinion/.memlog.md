- (direction) Driving inputs: prd.md (final, prd-secopinion-2026-09-20) and ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md (final, architecture-secopinion-2026-09-22). Both adopted as companions (not sources) - downstream (epics/stories, build) still needs full FR-level testable-consequence detail from the PRD and the full AD-n Binds/Prevents/Rule detail from the spine; neither is fully absorbable into the lean 5-field kernel without losing load-bearing detail.
- (decision) Why (from PRD Vision, S1): Healthcare has a structural conflict of interest - some multi-specialty hospitals/doctors are incentivized toward surgery/major treatment because it is revenue, not always because it is the best path for the patient. Patients rarely have the expertise or means to challenge a recommendation in the moment; getting a genuinely independent second opinion today costs another hospital visit and consultation fee. SecOpinion exists to make an independent, paid, document-based second opinion fast and accessible before a patient commits to major treatment - a vision-to-realize combined with a mandate the founder accepts: real money and real medical documents flow through v1 even though it is scoped as a POC.
- (capability) CAP-1 (mirrors PRD FR-1): intent - a Patient can register via mobile number + OTP. success - OTP-verified mobile number creates a Patient account; OTP delivery goes through OtpProvider (mocked v1, per ARCHITECTURE-SPINE AD-7).
- (capability) CAP-2 (mirrors PRD FR-2): intent - a Patient sets up a password credential after OTP verification, completing registration. success - a registered Patient can subsequently log in via mobile+password (OTP-fallback login status is an open question, see Open Questions).
- (capability) CAP-3 (mirrors PRD FR-3): intent - a Registrant can register a Case on behalf of a minor as their guardian. success - a guardian-registered account can submit Cases where the Patient is declared a minor, self-declared with no ID age verification in v1.
- (capability) CAP-4 (mirrors PRD FR-4): intent - a prospective Doctor can register with mobile+OTP, upload credential Document(s), and declare one or more Specialties (ARCHITECTURE-SPINE AD-12) plus a UPI payout method. success - registration is rejected without at least one credential Document and at least one declared Specialty; Doctor Verification Status starts Pending.
- (capability) CAP-5 (mirrors PRD FR-5): intent - the single Admin can manually review a Doctor's submitted credentials and set Doctor Verification Status to Verified or Rejected. success - a Rejected Doctor cannot access the case queue; a Verified Doctor gains access to Cases matching any of their declared Specialties (CAP-9). No automated third-party credential verification in v1.
- (capability) CAP-6 (mirrors PRD FR-6): intent - a Doctor provides a UPI ID as their payout destination during registration. success - Doctor Payouts (CAP-12) are disbursed to this UPI ID; only basic format validation in v1, no real-time bank/NPCI verification.
- (capability) CAP-7 (mirrors PRD FR-7): intent - a Patient describes their condition/treatment recommendation and uploads supporting Documents to a Case. success - at least one Document is required before a Case can proceed to payment; access control on uploaded Documents follows ARCHITECTURE-SPINE AD-14 (Cases-service-boundary enforcement, never in StorageProvider).
- (capability) CAP-8 (mirrors PRD FR-8): intent - a Patient selects Standard (Rs.1,000) or Urgent (Rs.2,000) urgency and pays before the Case enters a Doctor queue. success - Case remains unsubmitted/invisible to Doctors until payment succeeds; payment goes through PaymentProvider (mocked v1, real UPI gateway v2 - AD-3); Urgency Tier is fixed for the Case's life, no mid-case escalation (v2 item).
- (capability) CAP-9 (mirrors PRD FR-9): intent - a Verified Doctor views a queue of Cases matching any of their declared Specialties, with unassigned Cases claimable and urgent Cases prioritized/distinguished. success - a Doctor cannot see Cases outside their declared Specialties; claim is atomic (ARCHITECTURE-SPINE AD-6, optimistic locking via assignmentLockVersion) so two Doctors cannot claim the same Case; each Case shows time remaining against the Response SLA.
- (capability) CAP-10 (mirrors PRD FR-10): intent - a Doctor can request specific additional Documents from the Patient without closing the Case. success - Case Status moves to More Info Requested (ARCHITECTURE-SPINE AD-13 state machine) and the Response SLA clock pauses (AD-11, timestamp-pair shape); uploading the requested Document(s) returns status to Under Review and resumes the clock.
- (capability) CAP-11 (mirrors PRD FR-11): intent - a Doctor submits a written Opinion, closing the Case and making it visible to the Patient. success - a closed Case cannot be reopened by the Doctor (post-closure clarification channel is an open question); submitting an Opinion while Under Review or More Info Requested triggers Doctor Payout calculation (CAP-12); a Case in Refund Pending blocks Opinion submission entirely (race condition structurally eliminated).
- (capability) CAP-12 (mirrors PRD FR-12): intent - a Doctor is paid their Payout share of the Case fee (Platform Fee deducted first) after closing a Case with an Opinion within the Response SLA. success - payout is batched via a daily job (ARCHITECTURE-SPINE AD-10), not synchronous with Case closure; disbursed via PaymentProvider to the Doctor's registered UPI ID; amounts stored in a currency-safe representation (no float arithmetic); exact platform/doctor split percentage is an open question (20-30% platform range confirmed, exact value not yet set).
- (capability) CAP-13 (mirrors PRD FR-13): intent - if no Doctor submits an Opinion within the 5-day/120h Response SLA, the Case moves to Refund Pending and the Admin is notified to manually approve or reject a refund. success - clock is paused-adjusted (AD-11) and evaluated once per day (AD-8); Refund Pending locks the Case (no Opinion, no further Patient uploads) pending Admin action; Admin-approve refunds in full and closes the Case (Refunded, no Payout); Admin-reject leads to a manual phone contact - continuing grants a fresh 5-day/120h window with no v1 cap on retry cycles.
- (capability) CAP-14 (mirrors PRD FR-14): intent - a Document uploaded against a Case is viewable only by that Case's Patient and its currently assigned Doctor (or Admin). success - access control enforced per ARCHITECTURE-SPINE AD-14, at the Cases module service boundary, never inside StorageProvider or a route handler; standard HTTPS/TLS in transit; no encryption-at-rest or formal compliance program in v1 (explicit PM-flagged risk, see Constraints).
- (constraint) Constraints (bending design decisions): (1) Modular Monolith paradigm, module isolation, no cross-module DB joins/FKs (AD-1/AD-2/AD-4). (2) Payment, OTP, and file-storage integrations go through provider interfaces with v1 mocks and v2 real swaps (AD-3/AD-7) - no real gateway integration work in v1. (3) SLA breach detection and Doctor Payout are both daily batch jobs, not real-time (AD-8/AD-10) - sub-hour precision is explicitly not required. (4) Case status is a single formal state machine (AD-13) shared by all modules - no module may invent its own state names. (5) v1 is POC-scoped for security/compliance - no formal encryption-at-rest, DPDP/HIPAA-equivalent program, or data-residency commitment; carries an explicit PM-flagged risk given real medical documents and real payments flow through v1. (6) Single Admin role only - no multi-admin RBAC in v1. (7) Deployment target is AWS EKS as a single Deployment/Service (no per-module services in v1). (8) Stack is pinned: Next.js 16.3.6, Node.js 26 LTS, PostgreSQL 18.6, Prisma ORM v7.
- (decision) Non-goals (from PRD S5, explicit): not a telemedicine/live-consultation platform - no video/voice/chat between Patient and Doctor, document + written-opinion only. Not an emergency/urgent-care triage service - Urgent tier means expedited written review, not immediate medical attention. Not a multi-opinion marketplace in v1 - one Opinion per Case, one Doctor per Case, no doctor shopping/ratings/multiple-bids flow. No mid-case Standard-to-Urgent escalation (v2 item). No specialty-based auto-assignment/routing in v1 (ARCHITECTURE-SPINE AD-6) - only doctor self-claim and Admin assign/reassign. No formal security/compliance program in v1 (see Constraints).
- (decision) Success signal (from PRD S7 Success Metrics SM-1..SM-4 plus counter-metrics): SecOpinion v1 succeeds if a meaningful share of submitted Cases receive a Doctor Opinion within the 5-day/120h Response SLA (low SM-4 refund rate), Doctors return to review additional Cases after their first (retention, SM-3), and time-to-Opinion trends down over time (SM-2) - all without loosening the manual Doctor-verification bar to inflate doctor supply (counter-metric SM-C1) or doctors rushing low-quality Opinions to beat the SLA clock (counter-metric SM-C2). Demonstrable via: a cohort of real Cases moving Submitted through Opinion Given/Refunded, refund-rate and time-to-Opinion trending as intended, and Doctor Verification approval rate staying stable rather than being pushed up artificially.
- (assumption) Capability numbering CAP-1..CAP-14 was chosen to mirror PRD FR-1..FR-14 one-to-one for traceability, since the PRD's own functional-requirement breakdown already maps cleanly to independently reviewable capabilities - no re-grouping was needed.
- (question) Open questions inherited from PRD/Architecture, still unresolved: (1) Doctor specialty model - fixed enum list vs free text (CAP-4/CAP-9). (2) OTP-fallback login supported alongside password, or password-only (CAP-2). (3) Is zero-document Case submission ever allowed (CAP-7). (4) Supported Document formats - is DICOM needed or is JPEG/PNG/PDF sufficient (CAP-7). (5) Exact Platform Fee / Doctor Payout split percentage within the 20-30% platform range (CAP-12). (6) Post-closure clarification channel between Patient and Doctor, or is the written Opinion final (CAP-11). (7) Platform uptime SLA target, deployment scaling specifics, and observability/backup-DR policy - all explicitly deferred pre-production items in ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md Deferred section.
